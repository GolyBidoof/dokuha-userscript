Saves the book open in the k-manga viewer as a single ZIP of original-resolution page images, without turning a page. It reads the session out of the viewer URL, asks the viewer's own WebSocket for the page data, and unscrambles each page's block permutation on a canvas. Rename the ZIP to .cbz and any CBZ reader opens it.

  • Original-resolution pages, one file per page, rebuilt offline from the viewer's own data. The Mokuro hand-off files them as Series/Volume/page-0001.jpg. Free volumes need no account.
  • Reading stats before you commit: Natively level with JLPT band colours, ratings and reader counts, plus Manga Kotoba vocabulary totals.
  • Optional Mokuro OCR through the separate, locally running mokuro-bridge service. Pages stream across as they are rebuilt and come back as the .cbz / .mokuro / .webp trio that reader.mokuro.app reads, delivered to a local folder, MEGA, Google Drive, OneDrive, WebDAV or whichever other destination the bridge is configured for.
  • Resume, not restart: pages are cached as they arrive, an interrupted run continues where it stopped, and a partial failure names the pages that are missing.
  • Want the other four stores too? The all-in-one build adds BookWalker, CMOA, ebookjapan and Kindle.

Needs a userscript manager (Tampermonkey or Violentmonkey). For personal, lawful use: download only what you are entitled to access. MIT licensed. Source, issues and releases on GitHub.
